How to play Left-click a unit to select it, or drag a box. Shift adds. Ctrl+A selects everything.
Right-click: move; on an enemy: attack; on a rock, tree or wreck with builders: reclaim; on a half-built unit: help build.
Builders: pick something to build, then left-click the ground (shift to queue more). Right-click or Esc cancels.
Factories: click a unit to queue it (shift: 5), right-click to remove. Right-click the ground sets the rally point.
Enter opens the chat line for cheats: +atm, +nocost, +nowisee, +doubleshot, +halfshot.
S stops. Ctrl+D self-destructs what's selected after 5 seconds (press again to cancel). Swipe with two fingers (or arrow keys) to move the view; pinch or + / − to zoom. Space jumps to your selected units, or to your Commander; Ctrl+C selects your Commander and jumps to it.
