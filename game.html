Left-click a unit to select it, or drag a box. Shift adds. Ctrl+A selects everything.
Right-click: move; on an enemy: attack; on a rock, tree or wreck with builders: reclaim; on a half-built unit: help build.
Builders: pick something to build, then left-click the ground (shift to queue more). Right-click or Esc cancels.
Factories: click a unit to queue it (shift: 5), right-click to remove. Right-click the ground sets the rally point.
